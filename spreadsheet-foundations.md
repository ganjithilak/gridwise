# GRIDWISE — ROOT 1: SPREADSHEET FOUNDATIONS

## Purpose

Teach complete beginners what spreadsheets are, how they work, and how to navigate and organise information before introducing calculations.

The learner develops their skills through a continuous business scenario: **Golden Apples Farm**.

As spreadsheet skills develop, the farm's activities become increasingly interconnected.

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this root, learners should be able to:

1. Explain what a spreadsheet is.
2. Identify common uses for spreadsheets.
3. Recognise workbooks and worksheets.
4. Identify rows, columns, cells and ranges.
5. Navigate a spreadsheet.
6. Enter and edit basic information.
7. Organise information into a logical structure.
8. Sort information accurately.

## LESSON 1 — WELCOME TO GOLDEN APPLES FARM

### Introduction

Welcome to Golden Apples Farm!

You've recently taken over a small farm that grows fresh fruit and vegetables.

Your business has three main activities:

- **Growing produce:** Keeping track of crops and harvests.
- **Selling produce:** Managing sales at your roadside stall.
- **Purchasing supplies:** Tracking seeds, fertiliser, equipment and other expenses.

As your farm grows, you'll need to keep track of more information.

That's where spreadsheets come in.

### TEACH 1 — What Is a Spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet is a digital tool used to organise, store, calculate and analyse information.

Think of it as a notebook made up of rows and columns.

Unlike a paper notebook, a spreadsheet can also perform calculations, organise information and automatically update results when information changes.

**On-screen demonstration:**

Display a spreadsheet containing:

| Product | Quantity (kg) | Price per kg |
|---|---:|---:|
| Carrots | 100 | $2.50 |
| Potatoes | 200 | $3.00 |
| Tomatoes | 50 | $6.00 |

**Narration:**

"This spreadsheet shows three vegetables grown on your farm."

"It tells us what we're growing, how much we have and how much each product sells for."

"Each piece of information has its own place."

### PRACTICE 1 — Understanding Information

**Question:**

Which of the following could you use this spreadsheet to find out?

A. How many kilograms of carrots are available.

B. Whether it will rain tomorrow.

C. How many customers will visit next week.

**Correct answer:** A.

**Feedback:**

"Correct! The spreadsheet records that we have 100 kg of carrots."

"Spreadsheets help us work with information we've recorded. They don't automatically know information we haven't provided."

### TEACH 2 — Different Activities, Connected Information

Your farm also operates a roadside stall.

The stall sells vegetables harvested from your fields.

**On-screen demonstration:**

**Farm inventory**

| Product | Harvested (kg) |
|---|---:|
| Carrots | 100 |
| Potatoes | 200 |
| Tomatoes | 50 |

**Roadside stall**

| Product | Stock received (kg) |
|---|---:|
| Carrots | 40 |
| Potatoes | 80 |
| Tomatoes | 20 |

**Narration:**

"Your farm grows vegetables, but your roadside stall sells them."

"These are different activities within the same business."

"When vegetables are moved to the stall, the amount remaining at the farm changes."

"As your spreadsheet skills improve, you'll learn how to connect this information so changes can be calculated automatically."

### PRACTICE 2 — Recognising Relationships

**Question:**

Your farm harvests 100 kg of carrots.

You send 40 kg to the roadside stall.

How many kilograms remain at the farm?

A. 140 kg

B. 60 kg

C. 40 kg

**Correct answer:** B.

**Feedback:**

"Exactly! There are 60 kg of carrots remaining at the farm."

"Later, you'll learn how to make a spreadsheet calculate this automatically."

### APPLY — Choosing the Right Information

**Scenario:**

You want to know how much money Golden Apples Farm has spent purchasing seeds.

You have three spreadsheets:

1. Farm Inventory — Records harvested vegetables.
2. Roadside Sales — Records vegetables sold to customers.
3. Farm Expenses — Records purchases and operating costs.

**Question:**

Which spreadsheet would you examine?

**Correct answer:** Farm Expenses.

**Feedback:**

"Well done! Farm Expenses records the money spent operating your farm."

"Different spreadsheets can organise different kinds of information."

### LESSON RECAP

You've learned that:

1. Spreadsheets organise and store information.
2. Spreadsheets can perform calculations and analyse data.
3. Different business activities require different information.
4. Information from different activities can be connected.
5. As your skills develop, you'll learn to automate calculations and updates.

### COMPLETION

Congratulations!

You've completed your first lesson at Golden Apples Farm.

Your next lesson introduces the building blocks of every spreadsheet:

**Rows, columns and cells.**
